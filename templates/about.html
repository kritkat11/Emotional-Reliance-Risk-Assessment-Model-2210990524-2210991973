About ERRAM

Responsible Usage Monitoring of Conversational AI using Emotional Reliance Risk Assessment Model

Project Overview

Institution: Chitkara University, Rajpura, Punjab

Dataset: Emotions Dataset for NLP — View on Kaggle ↗

How It Works

  1. User enters text describing their feelings
  2. Text is cleaned — lowercased, punctuation removed
  3. TF-IDF converts text into 8,000 numerical features
  4. Logistic Regression predicts Low, Medium or High risk
  5. If confidence is below 55%, a warning is shown

Risk Categories

🟢 Low Risk

Normal, positive or neutral messages. No distress detected.

🟠 Medium Risk

Signs of stress, anxiety or loneliness detected.

🔴 High Risk

Severe distress or hopelessness. Immediate support recommended.

Technology Stack

Python · Flask · scikit-learn · TF-IDF · Logistic Regression · pandas · HTML5 · CSS3 · JavaScript
